# Patient Data Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

Document ID: POL-PRI-003
Effective Date: 2025-02-01
Department: Compliance & Health Information Management

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how staff access, use, disclose, and protect
patient health information across paper and electronic systems.

## 2. Minimum Necessary Access

Staff may access only the patient information necessary to perform
their specific job function ("minimum necessary" standard). Access to
the electronic health record (EHR) is logged, and random audits are
conducted monthly by Compliance to detect access by staff not involved
in a patient's care ("snooping").

## 3. Disclosure to Family Members

Clinical staff may share general health information with a patient's
identified support person or family member present for care
discussions, unless the patient objects. Detailed diagnosis, billing,
or treatment information may only be shared with individuals the
patient has explicitly authorized in writing, or as permitted under
applicable health privacy law for care coordination.

## 4. Requests for Records

Patients (or their legal representative) may request a copy of their
medical record through Health Information Management (HIM). Standard
turnaround time is 5 business days for electronic records and 10
business days for records requiring retrieval from archive. A
government-issued photo ID is required to release records in person.

## 5. Research and Quality Improvement Use

De-identified data may be used for internal quality improvement
without additional patient consent. Any research use involving
identifiable patient data requires Institutional Review Board (IRB)
approval and, in most cases, documented patient consent.

## 6. Data Breach Response

Any suspected unauthorized access or disclosure of patient information
must be reported to the Compliance Officer within 1 hour of discovery.
Compliance will conduct a risk assessment within 24 hours to determine
whether breach notification to affected patients and regulators is
required.

## 7. Photography and Recording

Photography or video/audio recording of patients by staff for
non-clinical purposes (e.g. social media, training materials) requires
separate written patient consent beyond the general treatment consent
form.

## 8. Third-Party Vendors

Any external vendor with access to patient data (billing services, lab
partners, IT contractors) must have an executed Business Associate
Agreement (or local equivalent) on file with Compliance before access
is granted.
