# Billing and Insurance Policy

Document ID: POL-BIL-009
Effective Date: 2024-09-01
Department: Patient Financial Services

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how the hospital verifies insurance coverage,
handles billing, and supports patients who need financial assistance.

## 2. Insurance Verification

Patient Financial Services verifies insurance eligibility and benefits
before any scheduled (non-emergency) admission or procedure. For
emergency admissions, verification occurs within 24 hours of
admission and does not delay treatment.

## 3. Accepted Insurance Providers

The hospital maintains network agreements with several major payers,
including Blue Cross, Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare.
Patients with out-of-network insurance are informed of potential
out-of-pocket costs at registration and may request a financial
counseling consultation.

## 4. Pre-Authorization

Certain procedures, particularly elective surgeries, advanced imaging,
and extended inpatient stays, require pre-authorization from the
patient's insurance provider. The scheduling team is responsible for
confirming pre-authorization status at least 48 hours before a
scheduled procedure; procedures may be postponed if authorization is
not on file.

## 5. Patient Billing Statements

An itemized billing statement is issued within 10 business days of
discharge, followed by monthly statements until the balance is
resolved or a payment plan is established. Patients may request an
itemized bill explanation from a financial counselor at any time.

## 6. Financial Assistance Program

Patients whose household income falls below 300% of the federal
poverty level may qualify for full or partial financial assistance
("charity care"). Applications are processed by Patient Financial
Services within 30 days and may be submitted up to 240 days after the
first post-discharge billing statement.

## 7. Payment Plans

Patients unable to pay a balance in full may request an interest-free
payment plan of up to 24 months, arranged through Patient Financial
Services. No patient is referred to collections while an active,
current payment plan or a pending financial assistance application is
on file.

## 8. Disputed Charges

Patients who believe a charge is incorrect may request a formal
billing review. Reviews are completed within 15 business days, and the
disputed balance is placed on hold pending the outcome.
