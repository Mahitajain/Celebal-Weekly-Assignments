# Visitor Policy

Document ID: POL-VIS-001
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Department: Patient Experience & Nursing Administration

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who may visit patients, when, and under what
conditions, in order to balance patient recovery, family involvement,
and infection control across all inpatient units.

## 2. General Visiting Hours

Standard visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Intensive Care
Unit (ICU) and Neonatal ICU visiting hours are restricted to 11:00 AM
to 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, with a maximum of two visitors at
the bedside at any time. Visitors arriving outside posted hours must
check in with the charge nurse and may be asked to wait.

## 3. Number of Visitors

A maximum of two visitors per patient is permitted at the bedside at
one time on general wards. Pediatric and maternity units allow up to
three visitors, including siblings, at the discretion of the unit
charge nurse. Isolation rooms are limited to one visitor at a time.

## 4. Restricted or Extended Visitation

Visitation may be restricted for patients in isolation due to
infectious disease, patients in active resuscitation, or during
active outbreaks as declared by Infection Control. Conversely,
end-of-life and critically ill patients may have extended or
24-hour visitation approved by the attending physician and charge
nurse, documented in the patient's chart.

## 5. Minors as Visitors

Visitors under 12 years of age must be accompanied by an adult at all
times and are not permitted in the ICU, oncology ward, or any
isolation room unless pre-approved by the attending physician.

## 6. Visitor Health Screening

All visitors must complete a brief symptom screening at the entrance
during flu season (October through March) or during any declared
outbreak period. Visitors reporting fever, cough, or gastrointestinal
symptoms within the prior 48 hours will be asked not to visit and may
be offered a virtual visit instead.

## 7. Identification and Sign-In

All visitors must sign in at the unit reception desk and wear a
visitor badge visible at all times. Badges must be returned at
sign-out. Overnight visitors (one per patient, general wards only)
must register with the charge nurse and receive an overnight visitor
pass.

## 8. Patient's Right to Restrict Visitors

Patients (or their designated healthcare proxy) may at any time
request that specific individuals not be permitted to visit. Such
requests must be documented in the patient's chart by nursing staff
and will be enforced by security if necessary.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions to any provision of this policy require approval from the
unit charge nurse or nursing supervisor and should be documented in
the patient's chart with the reason for the exception.
